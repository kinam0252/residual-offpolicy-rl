Object State Noise Robustness

Residual actor completely ignores object_state input. Even 200mm position noise or 90° rotation noise has zero impact on SR. The actor relies entirely on robot state (10D) + base_action (7D) from GR00T.

Why Object State Is Ignored

Actor input is [observation.state(10D) + object_state(7D) + base_action(7D)] = 24D. Despite object_state being concatenated, the actor learns near-zero weights for those 7 dimensions because:

  • GR00T's base_action already encodes cube position information (from depth vision)
  • Robot proprioception (observation.state) provides sufficient context for correction
  • The residual actor's job is: "given robot state + GR00T's action → how to correct it"

Analysis: Why v81 (Normal) Oscillates

  • High variance from randomization: Each eval samples different random positions, so SR fluctuates ±15-20% between evals
  • Yaw ±10° adds difficulty: GR00T's base policy drops from ~32% to ~2% with yaw — residual must compensate for both position AND rotation
  • Offline data mismatch: Offline data was collected on fixed v2 table (no random, no yaw) — doesn't match the random training distribution
  • pretrain_only mode: After critic warmup, switches to pure online — early good performance may come from offline pretrain, then degrades as online data dominates

Analysis: Why v82 (Hard) Collapses

  • Too wide range: dx[-12,+16]cm × dy[-20,+20]cm exceeds GR00T's training distribution (dx[-3,+11], dy[-13,+20])
  • Large yaw (±30°): Base policy is essentially random at these angles — residual can't learn meaningful corrections from noise
  • Catastrophic forgetting: Peaked at 90% @8K then steadily declined — online data from hard positions overwrites good policy

Potential Improvements

  • Curriculum learning: Start with easy range, gradually expand to normal
  • Collect offline data with random placement: Current offline data is from fixed tables only
  • off_frac=0.2 instead of pretrain_only: Keep mixing offline data throughout training for stability
  • Increase eval episodes: 20 envs × 3 episodes = 60 evals per checkpoint (currently 10×2=20)
  • Target tau: Lower tau (0.005) may stabilize the policy as shown in Round 2

Training Pattern

Most experiments follow a dip-then-recover pattern: baseline ~40-48% at step 0, drops to 0-25% at 2K-6K as residual destabilizes base policy, then recovers to 90-100% at 8K-16K as critic learns accurate Q-values.
